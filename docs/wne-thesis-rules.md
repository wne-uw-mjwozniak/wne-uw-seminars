## The seminar and the supervisor

- The teacher running the seminar is also the thesis supervisor.
- Changing seminar is possible from the next semester; mid-semester only in exceptional cases with
  the consent of the head of the teaching unit.
- Students going on Erasmus+ can suspend participation: deregister for the stay and register again
  after returning.

## Requirements for a bachelor's thesis

- A clearly defined research goal and a research question or hypothesis.
- Shows the ability to do research using current domestic or international scholarly literature.
- Research tools consistent with the programme of study, or going beyond it.
- Either the student's own study or an in-depth, literature-based report. If literature-based, the
  construction and interpretation must be the author's own.
- Traditional form: up to about 50 standard pages (100,000 characters with spaces).
- On programmes taught in Polish the thesis may be written in Polish or English; on programmes
  taught in English, in English.
- The thesis may take the traditional form or the form of a scientific article.
- The title needs the supervisor's approval and must convey the scope of the thesis.

## Topic approval

1. Student and supervisor agree the topic. The supervisor checks that it fits the programme, the
   level of study and the leading discipline.
2. The supervisor submits the topic to the Dean's Office during the last semester of the seminar,
   **no later than 4 weeks before the planned submission of the thesis**.
3. The Teaching Council reviews topics within 14 days. It may ask for changes; the Dean's Office
   forwards the committee's comments to the supervisor, who usually revises the title or the
   abstract and submits the topic again. **No reply within two weeks means the topic is approved.**
4. **A thesis whose topic has not been approved cannot be entered into APD or admitted to defence.**
5. Only the supervisor fills in the Dean's Office form, from a university account. Students do not
   submit topics themselves. Send the supervisor your name as in USOS, your student number and your
   programme; wrong or swapped entries are the most common reason a submission fails.
6. An approved topic is a **working topic** and may be refined. If the thesis later changes so much
   that it addresses a different problem, the topic must be submitted again as a modification, and
   the same deadlines apply. A more specific version of the same problem does not need resubmission.

### What "fits the programme" means (Komunikat 1/2026)

A thesis fits when it is anchored in the programme's learning outcomes, addresses problems within
the leading discipline, uses methods appropriate to the programme **in service of the thesis goals
rather than merely borrowing tools from other disciplines**, and deals with research or applied
problems typical of the programme.

| Committee | Programmes | The thesis should |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Economics specialisations (international, social, enterprise, entrepreneurship), Business Analytics, Interdisciplinary Economic and Managerial Studies, International Economics, European Environmental Economics and Policy | analyse economic phenomena at micro or macro scale; build on economic theory or policy; pose a clear research problem grounded in the literature |
| 2 | Finance and Accounting; Finance, Investments and Accounting; Finance, International Investment and Accounting; Quantitative Finance | address finance or quantitative-finance topics (pricing, risk management, financial engineering, algorithmic strategies, portfolio optimisation, ...); statistical, econometric and machine-learning methods are welcome as long as they serve the financial problem; a generic "business" theme is not enough |
| 3 | Informatics and Econometrics; Econometrics and Data Science; Interdisciplinary Economic and Mathematical Studies; Data Science and Business Analytics | show independent, critical use of advanced quantitative methods; state the economic, social or financial context; interpret results in terms of economic or social problems |

Practical consequence for this seminar: the economic or financial problem should be visible in the
thesis title itself.

## Co-authorship

| Form | Student authors | Share of each student | Share of the supervisor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional | 2 | exactly 50% | none |
| Scientific article | 1 | at least 60% | at most 40% |
| Scientific article | 2 | at least 40% | at most 20% |

- A thesis can have at most two student authors.
- All authors declare their substantive and percentage contribution to each chapter.
- The supervisor must justify the co-authorship in the review.
- A thesis may reuse parts of the author's coursework from other courses, provided they are extended
  under supervision and declared in the thesis.

## Thesis in the form of a scientific article

- The article has the structure typical for its field, about 10 to 12 thousand words, formatted
  uniformly for a chosen journal, with its own bibliography and appendices.
- A **supplement** is mandatory: literature review, methodology and results in more depth. It guides
  the reviewer and demonstrates the student's contribution. It is written by the student(s) alone.
- Article and supplement do not have to be in the same language.
- The article does not have to be published before the defence.
- Layout: title page; signed declarations; abstract page; table of contents; a card "Scientific
  article"; the article; a card "Supplement"; the supplement; lists.

The template in [`templates/thesis/`](../templates/thesis/) implements this layout.

## Formal requirements (Załącznik B and D)

- Order: title page; signed declarations of supervisor and author(s); page with abstract (max. 800
  characters), keywords (max. 10 words), Erasmus code, subject classification and the title in the
  other language; table of contents; main text; bibliography; lists (abbreviations, tables,
  figures, appendices).
- Times New Roman or similar, 12 pt; line spacing 1.5; all margins 25 mm; paragraph indent 1 cm.
- Pages numbered from the title page (number 0, not shown), bottom centre.
- Table of contents lists all headings; at most three levels of subsections.
- Main chapters start on a new page; headings in bold.
- Tables and figures numbered from 1 through the whole thesis, each with a title and a source line
  (10 pt). Refer to them by number, not as "the table below".
- Citations in the text as (Kowalski, 2015) or (Nowak et al., 2020). Footnotes (10 pt, continuous
  numbering) supplement the text; they do not carry bibliographic references.
- Bibliography alphabetical, unnumbered, preferably APA. Internet sources include author, title,
  date, URL and access date.
- Theses in English: same layout; keywords also in Polish, in brackets under the English ones; the
  abstract page gives the Polish title.

## Submission and defence

1. Send the thesis file by e-mail to the Dean's Office, which opens the entry in APD. For June and
   September defences the deadline is strict to the hour: **14:00** in the 2026/27 schedule from
   the Dean's Office (the faculty web page still says 12:00; treat the earlier hour as the safe one).
2. Upload a searchable PDF to APD with the English title, abstract and keywords, then pass it to the
   supervisor for approval. File name: `TYPE-PESEL.pdf`, capitals, no spaces or Polish characters
   (type codes are in Załącznik C).
3. Clear your accounts: WNE IT lab, WNE library, BUW, language and PE units.
4. The supervisor runs the anti-plagiarism check in JSA and accepts the thesis after reviewing the report.
5. Reviewers are appointed. Supervisor and reviewer upload reviews to APD at least 3 days before the defence.
6. One printed copy (double-sided, first three pages single-sided, soft binding with a transparent
   front cover, with the AI declaration) reaches the Dean's Office no later than 2 days before the
   exam. A print in a plastic sleeve is not accepted.

The thesis must contain the supervisor's declaration, the author's declaration and the
[declaration on the use of AI systems](https://www.wne.uw.edu.pl/student/prace-dyplomowe),
all signed. The Dean's Office checks the first pages and the declarations and **does not accept a
thesis with missing signatures, missing declarations or a wrong title-page layout**; such a thesis
goes back to the student and misses the round. The supervisor must have read and approved the
complete thesis before it is submitted, so the full draft has to be in on time.

## Grading

Supervisor and reviewer score the thesis on the official review form (Załącznik A). Half grades are
allowed. The thesis grade is the arithmetic mean of their grades.

| Grade | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 5! | Outstanding: well beyond the programme, a real contribution to science |
| 5 | Very good: significant use of the programme's content, requirements fully met |
| 4 | Good: moderate use of the programme's content |
| 3 | Weak: sufficient use, requirements partly met |
| 2 | Fail: substantive errors leading to wrong conclusions |
